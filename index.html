One more thing before you go

A letter for you

Hey Somya, If you're reading this, thank you for taking the time to go through something I made just for you. I kept wondering what could possibly be a meaningful gift, and I realized the best thing I could give wasn't something ordered online — it was my time, my effort, and a little piece of my heart. It's strange how we got here. A match on Schoomze, a follow on Instagram, and somehow a call that was supposed to be short turned into almost two hours that didn't feel like two hours at all. Since then, the random chats, the teasing, the late-night conversations have quietly become one of my favorite parts of being alive. Before you, days simply passed. Now they leave behind little moments that somehow end up connected to you. A funny reel makes me think of you. A good song makes me wonder if you'd like it. Ordinary days somehow became brighter because you're somewhere in them. What I appreciate most is how effortless everything feels with you. I never feel like I have to pretend to be someone else. That's a rare feeling, and I genuinely treasure it. I like your laugh, the way you care without making a big deal of it, how excited you get over the smallest things, and how naturally comfortable you make people feel — including me. I don't know what tomorrow looks like, and this isn't me trying to make it into something bigger than it is. I just wanted you to know that our paths crossing has genuinely made my days better, and I'm grateful for every conversation, every smile, and every memory we've already made. I hope this little website makes you smile, because that's really all I wanted. Keep laughing, keep chasing your dreams, and keep being exactly who you are. Happy Girlfriend's Day, Somya. I'm really glad I got to know you. ❤

— always, me
